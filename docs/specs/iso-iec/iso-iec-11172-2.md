# ISO/IEC 11172-2: description from free sources

A description of the parts of ISO/IEC 11172-2 (*MPEG-1 video*, 1993) that the deliverables need, written for this project from free sources ([README](README.md)). It is not the standard.

## 1. Scope

The deliverables read the sequence header of MPEG-1 video: dimensions, aspect ratio, frame rate and bit rate.

## 2. Sequence header

ITU-T H.262, the MPEG-2 video standard, is free (`itu-t-h-262`, the 02/2012 text as Amendment 4). It defines the sequence header that MPEG-1 video also starts with (section 6.2.2.1, `sequence_header()`), and it says how the two are told apart: when the first sequence header of a sequence is not followed by a sequence extension, the stream conforms to MPEG-1 (section 6.3.1). Its annex D.9 lists every difference between MPEG-1 and MPEG-2 video. The fields of the sequence header (horizontal and vertical size, frame rate code, bit rate, VBV buffer size, constrained parameters flag) are read as H.262 gives them, with the differences of annex D.9.

## Gaps

- **The aspect ratio code.** In MPEG-1 the 4-bit `pel_aspect_ratio` gives the sample aspect ratio, while in MPEG-2 `aspect_ratio_information` gives the display aspect ratio (`itu-t-h-262`, annex D.9.8); H.262 does not give the MPEG-1 table, and no other free source of it was found. Until one is approved, the pixel aspect ratio of an MPEG-1 stream is not reported.
